I build machines that work with the body. At MIT I design soft robotics and therapeutic devices — chemical pressure generation for untethered wearables, programmable knitted orthoses, and the experimental tools to characterize them. Before MIT I earned my BS at Georgia Tech and interned at NASA's Johnson and Marshall Space Flight Centers.

Metamaterial Heart Pump — System ID

Identifying the dynamics of a 3D-printed metamaterial soft robotic ventricle in a mock circulatory loop. I built the servo-driven hydraulic piston pump that made input volume exactly known.

MIT2026

Continuous Compartment Pressure Monitor

A wearable, BLE-connected patch for continuous intracompartmental pressure monitoring, built with MGH clinicians — validated to ±1 mmHg at the diagnostic threshold.

MIT · MGH2026

RC Car Steering — Design for Manufacturing

Re-engineering an RC car steering system for batch production: 3D geometries flattened to waterjet-cut 2D profiles, toleranced machining only where it matters.

MIT2025

ExoTic — Smart Fish Exoskeleton

A non-invasive, sensor-laden exoskeleton for free-swimming fish: SLA-printed flexible harness, embedded flex sensors and IMU, under 5% of body weight.

Georgia Tech2025

ExoSkins — Programmable Knitted Orthoses
