I research medical soft robotics and try to solve the challenges of devices that must
work in highly constrained environments. My current work focuses on ways to provide
energy to implantable and untethered devices. I approach problems through the
principles of precision machine design, with a focus on manufacturability at scale.
I'm drawn to complicated problems at the boundaries of mechanical engineering, and my
goal is to question the conventional limits of robotics to build technology that
augments how we interact with the world and with our own biology.
